# Ferris

`ferris.g4` is a 360×240 packed 2 bits-per-pixel image (4 pixels/byte,
MSB-first, `0` black … `3` white) reduced from Karen Davis's
[rustacean-flat-happy](https://rustacean.net/assets/rustacean-flat-happy.png)
on [rustacean.net](https://rustacean.net/). Used under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Transparent and near-white pixels are white so the splash can skip them
(no box behind the crab). Opaque orange maps to dark gray; linework to
black.
